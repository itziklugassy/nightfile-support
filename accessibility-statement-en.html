Accessibility Statement

Nightfile (אחרי חצות)

Version 1.0 · Dated October 24, 2026 · Effective November 1, 2026

Privacy Policy · Terms of Use

The short version

  • The App supports VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, Reduce Motion, high contrast and large touch targets.
  • You can choose the font, text size, line spacing and color theme (night, true black or sepia).
  • There are known limitations, mainly in the visual content of the graphic interludes. They are listed in Section 5.
  • Having trouble? Contact our accessibility coordinator, Itzik Lugassy, at bestoftbest.service@gmail.com or +972-52-344-5266. We reply within 14 business days.

1.Our commitment

We believe fiction should be available to every reader, including people with disabilities. We designed Nightfile to work with the accessibility features built into iOS and iPadOS, and we keep improving it. This statement is published in line with Israel's Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Law, 5758-1998, and the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 5773-2013, and reflects our commitment to users in the United States and elsewhere.

2.Scope

This statement covers the Nightfile (אחרי חצות) app for iPhone and iPad, version 1.0 and later, and the legal pages site where the Terms of Use, Privacy Policy and this statement are published. It does not cover screens that Apple displays and operates, such as the App Store purchase sheet and subscription management.

3.Conformance target

The App is designed to meet Israeli Standard SI 5568, which is based on the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.0, at Level AA, to the extent applicable to a native app, together with Apple's accessibility Human Interface Guidelines. Where relevant we also follow WCAG 2.1, for example on touch target size and support for both screen orientations.

Accessibility was checked in a self-assessment by the operator, using VoiceOver, Dynamic Type at every size, Reduce Motion and contrast checks on iPhone and iPad. The App has not yet been audited by an independent certified accessibility auditor. Date of the most recent check: October 27, 2026.

4.Accessibility features

Screen reader

  • All buttons, tabs and controls have VoiceOver labels in Hebrew and English.
  • Chapter text is real text, not an image, so VoiceOver and Spoken Content can read it aloud.
  • Narrator captions in the graphic interludes are presented as text and can be read aloud.
  • Reading and navigation order follow the language direction: right to left in Hebrew, left to right in English.

Text and display

  • Dynamic Type support, including the larger accessibility sizes.
  • A choice of three fonts per language, including a sans-serif option (IBM Plex Sans), and adjustable line spacing.
  • Three color themes: night, true black (OLED) and sepia. Text contrast is at least 4.5:1.
  • No information is conveyed by color alone.

Motion and media

  • When iOS Reduce Motion is on, interlude animation is turned off and video clips are replaced by a still image.
  • Video clips are muted by default and convey no information through sound alone.
  • Interludes do not advance without your action, and the App has no time limits.

Operation

  • Touch targets are at least 44 by 44 points.
  • The close button on the subscription screen is always visible.
  • Your reading position is saved automatically, and you can add bookmarks and read offline.

Legal pages site

  • The pages use semantic HTML with headings, marked-up tables and a skip link, and adapt to screen size and browser text size.
  • The pages follow your device's light or dark mode setting.

5.Known limitations

Despite our efforts, some parts of the App may not be fully accessible. These are the limitations we know about:

  • Illustrations and video clips. The illustrated panels and video clips in the interludes are visual content. Narrator captions are available as text, and every panel also has a text description that VoiceOver reads aloud.
  • No built-in audio narration. This version has no narration. You can use VoiceOver or iOS Spoken Content to listen to the text.
  • Very large text sizes. At the largest accessibility sizes, long titles in the library may be truncated with an ellipsis. The full text is available to VoiceOver and on the story page.
  • Apple screens. The purchase sheet, subscription management and restore flow are operated by Apple, which is responsible for their accessibility.
  • Cognitive accessibility. The stories are adult fiction written in literary language with complex plots. We do not currently offer a plain-language edition.

6.Turning on accessibility features

  • VoiceOver: Settings > Accessibility > VoiceOver.
  • Text size: Settings > Accessibility > Display & Text Size > Larger Text. You can also change text size in the App's reader settings.
  • Reduce Motion: Settings > Accessibility > Motion > Reduce Motion.
  • Spoken Content: Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content.

7.Contact us about accessibility

If you run into a barrier, if something does not work with the assistive technology you use, or if you have a suggestion, we want to hear from you. On request we can also provide the text content of the interludes in an accessible format.
