Terms of Use · Accessibility Statement

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up. The app never asks for your name, email, phone number or location. We only get an email address if you choose to write to us.
  • What you read stays on your device. Reading progress, bookmarks, display settings, language and notification preference are stored on your device and are never sent to us.
  • Apple handles payment. We never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions; it receives a random, anonymous ID and your purchase records.
  • No ads, no analytics tools, no tracking, no cookies. The only analytics are aggregate subscription metrics in the RevenueCat dashboard (Section 17). We do not access your advertising identifier (IDFA), and we do not sell or share personal information.
  • Story downloads come from a static content server. As with any web request, the hosting provider may log your IP address and device type.
  • Questions or requests: bestoftbest.service@gmail.com. We reply within 14 business days.

This summary is for convenience only. The full text below governs.

4.Information we do not collect

The App does not collect, request or access:

  • your name, email address, phone number or postal address (we only get an email address if you write to us);
  • your Apple Account details. Apple does not give us your name or email address;
  • card numbers or any other payment details;
  • location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data;
  • your advertising identifier (IDFA), browsing history, or data from other apps and websites;
  • your date of birth or age. The App's age rating is set on the App Store; the App does not ask you to enter your age.

We do not process "sensitive personal information" as defined in the CCPA, or "information of special sensitivity" under Israeli law. If you include such information in a message to us, we use it only to reply and delete it promptly.

5.Why we use information

We process information only to:

  • run the App and download chapters, illustrations and video clips;
  • verify subscriptions, handle renewals and cancellations, and restore purchases;
  • answer messages, handle privacy and consumer requests, and resolve disputes;
  • keep the service secure, prevent fraud, and prevent circumvention of the subscription;
  • meet legal obligations, including tax and bookkeeping rules.

We do not use information for advertising, targeted marketing, profiling, automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects, or training artificial intelligence models. We do not send marketing messages.

8.Retention and deletion

We keep information only as long as needed for the purpose for which we received it, or as the law requires. Retention periods are listed in Section 3.

Data on your device

Deleting the App deletes your reading data, bookmarks and settings. We have no copy and cannot restore them. Note that a device backup (for example iCloud) may include this data, depending on your backup settings.

Deleting the App does not cancel your subscription

Apple manages your subscription and it renews automatically until you cancel. To cancel: Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions, or in the App go to Settings > "Manage subscription". Full details are in Section 10 of the Terms of Use.

Deleting data at RevenueCat

RevenueCat stores the anonymous ID and purchase data on our behalf. To delete them, email bestoftbest.service@gmail.com. Because we do not know who you are, include something that lets us find the record, such as the Order ID shown on the receipt Apple emailed you. We will use it only to locate the record, delete it and confirm. If you later tap "Restore Purchases", a new record may be created. Apple keeps your purchase history under its own policy.

Messages

Correspondence is kept for 24 months after the request is closed, unless we need it for legal protection or the law requires longer. You may ask for earlier deletion.

9.Security

The best protection for information is not collecting it, so the App is built on data minimization. In addition:

  • communication between the App and servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS);
  • local data is kept in the App's private storage and protected by iOS security features;
  • the content server only serves static files and does not receive information from the App for storage;
  • access to the Apple, RevenueCat, hosting and email admin consoles is limited to the operator and protected by two-factor authentication;
  • we take reasonable measures consistent with Israel's Privacy Protection (Data Security) Regulations, 5777-2017, where they apply, at a level suited to the nature of the information.

No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Given how little we hold, the risk to your privacy is low.

10.Security incidents

If a security incident may affect personal information, at our end or at a provider, we will act to contain it and assess its impact; report to Israel's Privacy Protection Authority where the law requires; notify affected users where the law requires, including under U.S. state breach notification laws where they apply; and document the incident and our response. Because we hold no contact details, notices will usually be posted in the App and on the legal pages site. Found a security vulnerability? Please tell us at bestoftbest.service@gmail.com.

15.Cookies and tracking

  • The App does not use cookies, pixels or similar tracking technologies.
  • The legal pages site sets no cookies and contains no analytics, advertising or embedded third-party content. The hosting provider may record technical browsing data in server logs, as described in Section 6.
  • The local storage the App uses for reading progress and settings is necessary for the service you asked for, is not read by any third party and is not used for tracking.
  • There is no "tracking" as Apple defines it. We do not link App data with third-party apps or websites, and we do not access the IDFA, so the App shows no App Tracking Transparency prompt.

For these reasons we do not display a cookie notice or consent banner. If we ever start using non-essential cookies, we will update this policy and ask for consent where the law requires.

16.iOS permissions and notifications

The only permission the App may request is notifications, and only if you choose to turn on reminders. Notifications are local: they are scheduled on your device itself (for example, on a new chapter's release day), and we do not collect or store a push token. You can turn them off at any time in the App or in iOS Settings. The App does not request access to location, camera, microphone, photos or contacts.
