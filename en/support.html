Restore purchases

  1. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account that bought the subscription.
  2. Open the app and tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen or in Settings.
  3. If nothing is found, check that the subscription is active in Apple's subscription settings, then email us.

Cancel a subscription

  1. On iPhone or iPad: Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Nightfile > Cancel Subscription.
  2. Or from the app: Settings > Manage subscription.
  3. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.
  4. You can also send us a cancellation notice by email, phone or mail, with the Order ID from your Apple receipt.

After you cancel in Apple's settings, access continues until the end of the paid period or free trial.

Refunds

  • Apple processes all payments. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com; Apple decides under its policies.
  • Users in Israel may cancel at any time under the Consumer Protection Law. Ask us for immediate cancellation and we refund the pro rata part of your payment for the rest of the period within 14 days, with no cancellation fee.
  • Cancelling a distance purchase within 14 days of the transaction (and, for a subscription that began with a free trial, within 14 days of the first charge) gives a pro rata refund with no cancellation fee, where that right applies.
  • If you were charged for a renewal more than 3 business days after you sent us a cancellation notice (6 business days by registered mail) and Apple did not refund it, we refund it ourselves within 14 days.

The binding details are in Section 10, Section 11 and Section 12 of the Terms of Use.
